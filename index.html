A self-guided climb from shipping mobile apps to running engineering as a CTO. Start with 24 weeks of hands-on backend foundations, build out a mastery toolbox and deep-dive topics, then follow a five-stage roadmap through the technical, people, delivery and business skills the role really demands.

Named for Kaizen (改善) — the Japanese philosophy of continuous, incremental improvement. One small concept a day, compounding for the long climb. The goal isn't to finish; it's to keep leveling up.

Part 3 · The Road to CTO

The second half of the journey

The foundations build the engineer; this builds the chief. A CTO's job is mostly people, delivery, business and strategy — so 32 planned modules across 5 stages (weeks 25–96) cover the rest of the climb: finishing the technical foundation, then leading people, running delivery, business fluency, and the CTO craft itself. 2 shipped so far — The Gatekeeper (deep auth) and The First Follower (IC→manager).

Why it works

Built the Kaizen way

Small, consistent steps beat occasional heroic effort. The whole program is designed around momentum.

📱

Mobile-first analogies

Every new concept is anchored to something you already know — URLSession, Core Data, APNs, Podfiles.

⌨️

Type it, don't copy it

30 min reading, 30 min writing the code by hand. Muscle memory over passive reading.

🛠️

A project every week

Six shippable mini-projects that compound — each phase builds on the last one's output.

🧭

Architect's journal

An ongoing "how would I architect this?" habit that turns knowledge into judgement.

Daily rhythm

1–2 hours a day

The cadence that carries you across the whole journey — foundations and beyond — without burning out.
